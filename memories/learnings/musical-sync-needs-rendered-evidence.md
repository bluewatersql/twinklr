# Audio transport alignment does not establish musical synchronization

On 2026-11-19, waveform-aligned previews with full-song light coverage still
failed owner review: the house flashed without musical correspondence and missed
expected transitions. Investigation found an unconsumed pulse clock, fades that
restarted inside beat windows, a CUT that still faded, and timed musical evidence
removed before house planning. [Dated evidence](../../changes/2026_11_12_mh_true_up/evidence/2026-11-19-house-musical-sync/README.md).

Keep the claims separate: soundtrack alignment establishes transport; a trace
establishes scheduling; a rendered comparison establishes what light actually
changed at a named musical event. None supplies the owner's taste judgment.
For a music-response repair, retain a specific audio event, its planned response,
the emitted timing/envelope, and a rendered before/after. Tests should include
noncoincident bar/beat timestamps and frame rounding; an ideal uniform grid can
hide a missing first pulse or a rejected mandatory foundation.
